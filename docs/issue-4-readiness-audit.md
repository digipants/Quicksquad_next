# ResolveUS SEO, deployment, consent, and Google Ads readiness audit

Audit date: 2026-11-03

## November 3, 2026 — owner-approved billing and policy update (draft)
- Basic ($20/month), Premium ($75/month), Enterprise (custom) plan rates and feature lists are preserved.
- All website-initiated Razorpay checkout buttons and payment capture scripts were removed; plan CTAs now open a consultation enquiry. This website does not create a payment transaction record. Any agreed off-site payment and records are managed separately.
- `support@resolve-us.com` is the requested support/refund address. Eligible approved refunds are to be **issued within 14 calendar days after approval**. The owner still needs to verify mailbox receipt, financial operations, and the ability to fulfill the refund promise.
- Terms, Refund, Privacy and Disclaimer were rewritten to reflect the above; pricing, FAQ, contact and footer were aligned.
- No public-facing page claimed Google had prohibited the business from offering support; Google's independent third-party consumer tech-support advertising policy is an **internal paid-campaign eligibility concern** and is retained in this audit, not presented as a restriction on customers.
- Google Ads account verification, consent/CMP implementation, lead conversion tracking and hosted regression checks remain separate outstanding items. This draft does not confer eligibility or permit a paid launch.

## Scope and outcome

The original PR #5 pass addressed technical SEO claims and excluded generated Netlify state from source control. This November 3 addendum documents a separate, owner-approved payment-flow and policy change. Neither pass alone establishes Google Ads eligibility or regional privacy compliance.

The sitemap now lists the public routes that are known to exist without assigning a freshly generated `lastModified` to unchanged pages. Blog detail URLs are not listed yet: the current blog model has no publication state, and the site can serve local fallback content as well as database records. An owner must identify which database posts are approved for public indexing before those slugs can be added safely.

The site-wide JSON-LD keeps the organization, website, and service entities, identifies A V Food Factory as the operator, and no longer claims a worldwide service area. The global FAQ and home-only breadcrumb entities were removed because they were emitted on every route and did not describe route-specific visible content.

## Launch verification checklist

1. Inspect the failed `resolveus` Deploy Preview log and settings in Netlify. Establish whether the exit code 2 is caused by build configuration, application compilation, or another stage. Inspect the two cache matches safely; do not disable the secret scanner or whitelist `EMAIL_PASS` by name.
2. Generate a successful `resolveus` preview, review the primary and category/pricing/contact/legal pages, and verify HTTPS, status codes, canonical URLs, robots, sitemap, and structured data. Keep production deployment separate.
3. Have the owner decide which service categories are in scope for paid advertising. Do not advertise restricted consumer technical support or imply government affiliation, financial/medical licensing, or services that cannot be delivered.
4. Verify the actual Google Ads advertiser/business status, business name/logo assets, telephone asset, and any finance, healthcare, or government-specific certifications in the account.
5. Verify the enquiry-only workflow, plan prices and entitlements, off-site payment terms, invoice/receipt procedures, cancellations, refund operations, and availability of support@resolve-us.com. Do not reactivate site checkout without explicit approval.
6. Confirm intended markets and privacy requirements, review the implemented consent controls and their Privacy Policy description, and test the tag's consent states. Do not treat Consent Mode alone as a consent manager or as proof of legal compliance.
7. Use Tag Assistant and GA4 Realtime to verify the measurement ID and accepted-enquiry `generate_lead` event on a preview. Confirm the desired conversion imports and attribution settings in Google Ads separately.
8. Verify the ResolveUS domain property and submit the sitemap in Search Console. Add only confirmed published blog posts after a publication state is established.
9. Confirm the real phone routing, response hours, contact mailbox, refund process, and any required physical-address disclosure with the operating owner.
