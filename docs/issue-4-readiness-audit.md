# Resolve US SEO, deployment, consent, and Google Ads readiness audit

Audit date: 2026-11-03

## Scope and outcome

This repository pass addresses only technical SEO claims that can be verified from the code and excludes generated Netlify state from source control. It does not change consultation categories, service delivery, prices, checkout or payment configuration, customer records, analytics IDs, or advertising settings. It does not establish Google Ads eligibility or legal/privacy compliance.

The sitemap now lists the public routes that are known to exist without assigning a freshly generated `lastModified` to unchanged pages. Blog detail URLs are not listed yet: the current blog model has no publication state, and the site can serve local fallback content as well as database records. An owner must identify which database posts are approved for public indexing before those slugs can be added safely.

The site-wide JSON-LD keeps the organization, website, and service entities, identifies A V TRADE CORPORATION as the operator, and no longer claims a worldwide service area. The global FAQ and home-only breadcrumb entities were removed because they were emitted on every route and did not describe route-specific visible content.

## Launch verification checklist

1. Inspect the failed `resolveus` Deploy Preview log and settings in Netlify. Establish whether the exit code 2 is caused by build configuration, application compilation, or another stage. Inspect the two cache matches safely; do not disable the secret scanner or whitelist `EMAIL_PASS` by name.
2. Generate a successful `resolveus` preview, review the primary and category/pricing/contact/legal pages, and verify HTTPS, status codes, canonical URLs, robots, sitemap, and structured data. Keep production deployment separate.
3. Have the owner decide which service categories are in scope for paid advertising. Do not advertise restricted consumer technical support or imply government affiliation, financial/medical licensing, or services that cannot be delivered.
4. Verify the actual Google Ads advertiser/business status, business name/logo assets, telephone asset, and any finance, healthcare, or government-specific certifications in the account.
5. Confirm exact checkout entitlements, one-time versus recurring billing, receipts, refunds, and customer support operations. Do not enable live payments or change prices without separate approval.
6. Confirm intended markets and privacy requirements, select/implement the consent solution, describe actual behavior in the Privacy Policy, and test the tag's consent states. Do not treat Consent Mode alone as a consent manager.
7. After PR #3 and a successful `resolveus` preview, use Tag Assistant and GA4 Realtime to verify the new measurement ID. Add a privacy-safe, non-PII lead conversion only after notification email acceptance and duplicate handling are defined.
8. Verify the Resolve US domain property and submit the sitemap in Search Console. Add only confirmed published blog posts after a publication state is established.
9. Confirm the real phone routing, response hours, contact mailbox, refund process, and any required physical-address disclosure with the operating owner.
